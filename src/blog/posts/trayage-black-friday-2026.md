## A little less Downloads clutter

Downloads has a way of collecting things you only needed once: the installer for an app you already use, an old export, a ZIP file you opened weeks ago. Finding those files again should be easier than sorting through the whole folder and guessing what can go.

That’s where Trayage helps. It brings files worth reviewing into one place, gives you the details behind each suggestion, and leaves the decision with you.

## A clearer look at what’s in Downloads

Trayage is a Mac menu bar app for reviewing the files that build up in Downloads or another folder you choose. Smart Filters help narrow the list by details such as age, size and file type, so you can start with the files that matter to your review.

You can inspect a file or reveal it in Finder before deciding what to do. Suggestions are a starting point, not a verdict that a file is safe to delete. Likely duplicates are candidates based on filename and size, not a claim that their contents are identical.

Nothing is selected or removed automatically. You choose the files and confirm before they move to Trash, and Trayage never empties it. File analysis stays local to your Mac.
