Trayage is nominated in four categories at the **MacPip Awards 2026**, and I’d love your support:

- **Best Utility**
- **Best Free App**
- **Best Pay-Once App**
- **Hidden Gem**

These are community-voted awards. MacPip includes all eligible listed apps as nominees, and readers choose the winners. If Trayage has helped you give Downloads a second look, you can vote for it in each category for free before voting closes on **December 20, 2026**.

<p><a class="button" href="https://macpip.com/awards/2026">Vote for Trayage in the MacPip Awards →</a></p>

## How to vote

1. Open the [MacPip Awards 2026 voting page](https://macpip.com/awards/2026).
2. Sign up for a free MacPip account with your email, then enter the code sent to your inbox. If you already have an account, sign in.
3. Find **Trayage** under **Best Utility** and cast your vote. Repeat for **Best Free App**, **Best Pay-Once App**, and **Hidden Gem**, searching for Trayage within each category.

Each person gets **one vote per award**, so you can support Trayage in **all four categories**. You can change your votes before voting closes on **December 20, 2026**. MacPip will announce the winners on **December 21, 2026**.

You can also start with [Trayage’s Best Utility nomination](https://macpip.com/awards/2026/utilities/trayage). If that link opens the full awards page, search for Trayage under Best Utility.

## A little help with Downloads

If you’re new here, Trayage is a Mac menu bar app for reviewing the files that build up in Downloads or another folder you choose. It brings older files, large downloads, installers, and possible repeat downloads into a review, with details to help you decide what you still need.

File review is free, and file analysis stays on your Mac. You choose which files move to Trash and confirm before cleanup. Trayage never empties Trash.

You can [try Trayage for Mac]({{ base }}download/) or watch the [short walkthrough]({{ base }}#walkthrough) to see how a review works.

Thank you to everyone who has tried Trayage, shared feedback, or told someone about it. I build Trayage as a solo maker, and that support means a lot. If it has earned a place in your Mac routine, I’d appreciate [your votes](https://macpip.com/awards/2026).
